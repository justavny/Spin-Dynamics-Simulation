\section{Spin States}

The computational basis states are

\[
|0\rangle=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
|1\rangle=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\
1
\end{pmatrix}
\]

A general qubit state is

\[
|\psi\rangle=\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle
\]
